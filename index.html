a little about me

I'm finishing my master's in analytics at Georgia Tech this December. Before that I did my undergrad here in industrial engineering, which is basically four years of asking why something takes longer than it should.

That question has followed me into every job I've had. At a hospital it was provider paperwork. At UPS it was data pipelines nobody could see inside. At Rivian and Volkswagen it was developers checking build status by hand. The work looks different each time, but I always start the same way: watch how people actually work, then build the thing that saves them the trip.

I sit somewhere between engineering and product. I like writing the code, but I like figuring out what's worth building even more.

Admissions prediction for Lake Forest College

in progress

My master's practicum. We're helping a small college figure out which prospective students are likely to apply, from 630K+ CRM records where fewer than 1 in 300 people convert. Our first model scored suspiciously well, and it turned out four fields were quietly giving away the answer. Taking them out made the numbers look worse and the model actually useful. Right now the top 5% of prospects it ranks hold over half of the real conversions.
